Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Synth Wave Defense ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you play our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and are committed to minimizing data collection:

  • Gameplay & Local Data: Game progress, unlocked towers, levels, gems, and settings are saved locally on your device via standard local storage. We do not require account registration or store personal identifying data (like your name or email) on our own servers.
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: To help troubleshoot crashes and ensure stability, anonymous technical data (device model, operating system version, crash stack traces) may be logged locally or processed in aggregate form.
  • Advertising Data: The game utilizes third-party advertising SDKs (such as Yandex Mobile Ads) to show rewarded video advertisements. These third-party networks may collect anonymous identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and device metadata to deliver and optimize ads according to applicable platform guidelines.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Yandex Mobile Ads)

Our game displays voluntary rewarded video ads provided by the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. These ads allow players to earn in-game bonuses (such as reward multipliers or bonus items).

Yandex and its partners process data in accordance with their own privacy policy. You can review how Yandex handles data by visiting:

Yandex Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local threshold). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

4. Data Security & Retention

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, you can delete this data at any time by clearing the application data/cache in your Android system settings or uninstalling the app.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
